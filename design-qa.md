## Checks completed

- The title asset was generated from the game's `titles.sprite.skel` `torch_loop`, not reconstructed from a small set of raw atlas crops.
- The first high-density renderer still split each region quad as `(0,1,2)` plus `(1,3,2)`. With Spine's bottom-left, top-left, top-right, bottom-right vertex order, those crossed diagonals overlapped one portion of the quad and left a real central lower wedge unpainted. The corrected `(0,1,2)` plus `(0,2,3)` triangles restore the inner title baseline and the complete torch handle in every frame.
- The generated replacement contains 120 frames at the final 320 × 100 presentation size and covers the complete 12-second loop at 10 FPS.
- Layout bounds follow the title, outline, torch, fire, and sparks while allowing smoke to crop naturally. Each target-resolution frame reserves 14 horizontal and 5 vertical pixels around the layout bounds; this matches the normalized source's visual scale while retaining the full title baseline and torch handle.
- The earlier alpha-aware dark-tone floors and gamma/gain adjustments were removed. Region and mesh attachments now retain the source texture RGB, alpha, Spine tint, blend mode, and transforms; the black title interiors, red outlines, and dark iron torch are no longer recoloured. No runtime tint, blur, drop shadow, mask, or duplicate glow layer is present.
- The first palette-recovery pass still applied a hidden final-output `LiftTone` (`pow(0.84) × 1.06`) after compositing. Independent review identified that remaining source of excess brightness; the method and all calls were removed, and straight-alpha export now writes the composited source channels directly.
- The source `titles.sprite.png` stores premultiplied-alpha RGB: every nontransparent outline pixel has RGB channels no greater than alpha. The previous renderer treated those channels as straight RGB and multiplied them by texture alpha a second time; WPF then premultiplied the exported PNG again, suppressing the antialiased red outline while leaving opaque pixels largely unchanged. The corrected compositor carries the source premultiplied RGB through source-over blending once, then converts to straight BGRA exactly once for PNG export.
- The header consumes each 320 × 100 frame at its native logical size. The corrected same-size comparison shows that 320 × 100 contains enough pixels when alpha is handled correctly, so the header is not enlarged and high-quality scaling remains available only when the operating-system DPI requires an upscale.
